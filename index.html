About

Design, built and measured.

I'm Felipe, a product designer based in Bogotá, Colombia. I like owning the whole loop — from the first sketch in Figma to the shipped interface, and then back into the data to see if it actually worked.

AI is part of how I work, not just what I design. I use it to move faster from idea to working product — prototyping, building, and automating the parts that used to take days — so I can spend more time on the decisions that actually need a human.
